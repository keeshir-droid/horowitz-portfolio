## Projects shipped

Free, phone-first websites for young, expressive people. You do something real with your hands, your phone captures it, the site turns it into something delightful, you send it to someone, and they discover the site.

- **Shot Planner**: A Claude skill that turns a reel script into a shot list.
- **Handwriting Font**: Write a list on paper, take one photo, then type in your own handwriting. https://handwriting-font-converter.vercel.app
- **Doodle Alive**: Snap a notebook doodle and the ink lifts off the page and wobbles in a 6-second story. https://doodle-alive.vercel.app
- **Underline**: Photograph a book page and swipe a line. A real-looking highlighter marks it. https://underline-it.vercel.app
- **Look Up**: Photograph the sky, trace the shape you see in a cloud, send it as a postcard. https://look-up-sky.vercel.app
- **Pocket Booth**: A 4-shot photo-booth strip from your camera. Share it or print at true size. https://the-pocket-booth.vercel.app
- **Spotted a Cutie**: Snap a street animal and get a goofy collectible card made for one friend. https://spotted-a-cutie.vercel.app
