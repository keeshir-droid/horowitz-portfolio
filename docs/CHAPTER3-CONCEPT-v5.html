Chapter 3 · concept v5 · a different idea

High school is a series.

Rough concept frames, not the build. You said v4 was the worst, with weak visuals, and you asked how to make this cinematic and tied to pop culture. So I stopped drawing a school. Boarding school, coming of age, the most-used story in film and TV: Chapter 3 becomes a season of a prestige teen drama, using film and streaming language everyone already knows.

Part 1 · why this fits

Games, then film

  • The portfolio's logic. Chapter 1 is a Game Boy, Chapter 2 is an arcade. Each chapter is the pop culture you'd have been in at that age. At boarding school it's film and TV: the coming-of-age story.
  • Your own thread. Your visa entry says you wanted to study film and computer science. A cinematic chapter is you showing that side, not just describing it.
  • It's easy to follow. Anyone knows what a cold open, opening credits, episode select, "Skip intro" and "Next episode in 5…" mean. No explaining, and it's quick to skim.
  • The hook is built in. The season ends on a cliffhanger. That's the pet therapy attempt (the one thing that didn't work), then "TO BE CONTINUED", which flows into Chapter 4's "CONTINUE? 9…8…7".

Part 3 · the season

Seven episodes, about 45 seconds

  • E1 · Cold open. Boarding school. 1.5 s.
  • Opening credits. Honor societies, lesson plans, best-in-subject. ~5 s.
  • E2 · Carbon (big). Interactive meter. ~9 s.
  • E3 · Torque (big). Interactive motor. ~10 s.
  • E4 · Game On. The first official esports tournament, shown like a live sports broadcast: bracket, "LIVE" tag, a lower-third. ~3 s.
  • E5 · Encore. Performing: solo, music, a new field (stand-up paper). A concert-film moment: spotlight, crowd sound, one chord. ~3 s.
  • E6 · Zero AI (big). Film-style terminal. ~8 s.
  • E7 · Not Yet. Pet therapy, the cliffhanger. ~4 s.
