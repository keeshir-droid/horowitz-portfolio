Rough concept frames, not the build. Short answers first: no, it is not a house we zoom in and out of. That idea was confusing and it is gone. Your point about "the unimportant things are also there" was right, so this version has a simple rule: only the important things get screen time. Everything else is something you walk past.

Part 1 · the idea in three sentences

You walk one path, from dawn to night

  1. The camera moves forward only, along one path through the Highclerc campus (the high school, grades 9 to 12) in the Palani Hills, painted in layers so hills, mist and trees slide past at different speeds.
  2. The time of day changes as you scroll: blue dawn mist when you arrive, bright clear midday on the overlook, gold evening, then night with the windows lit. You can feel progress without reading anything.
  3. Only three stops take over the screen and become the thing itself (the carbon meter over the real valley view, the motor you can drag, the Java terminal). Everything else is a 2-second walk-by: a lit doorway with one line.
Why this is more stimulating than v3: the place does the work. A beautiful, changing, misty hill school is something people want to keep looking at, and the three big stops give you something to touch. The wide view never "zooms out"; you are always on the path.

The whole chapter as one strip. Dawn to night is the progress bar nobody has to read. The three amber stops get ~29 s of the chapter's ~48 s; every white stop is about 2 s.

Part 4 · the honest bit

I can't prove "stimulating" with sketches

Flat sketches can't show motion, mist or time of day changing, and that's exactly what this idea depends on. So instead of another concept page, I suggest a 10-second working slice: dawn arrival, the walk, and the classroom doorway, with real parallax and mist and the sound of the place. You scroll it in your browser and tell me if it makes you want to continue. If it doesn't, we change direction after spending a small amount, not a whole chapter.

What would make it feel truly like Kodai: 3 to 5 of your own photos of the campus (a building, the view, the path you walked), plus the names of 2 or 3 places you'd want in it. I won't invent building names or details. All the shapes above are placeholders.
