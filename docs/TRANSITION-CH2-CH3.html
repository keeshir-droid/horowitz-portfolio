Beat by beat · 4.2 s

  1. 0.0–0.5 s · Save, power down. Chapter 2's arcade screen: STAGE CLEAR, "SAVING… DO NOT TURN OFF", then it collapses to a line.
  2. 0.5–1.9 s · Disc in. A dim bedroom with a warm lamp. The console on the desk: the tray opens, a disc with a printed "CHAPTER 3 · HIGH SCHOOL" label drops in, the tray closes, and the LED goes from red to green.
  3. 1.9–2.2 s · TV on. The CRT switches on: a white line snaps open into a picture, the mirror of Chapter 2's power-down.
  4. 2.2–2.8 s · Memory card. "Load this data? RISHEEK · CHAPTER 2 · COMPLETE ▶ YES". Your save carries over, so the story is continuous.
  5. 2.8–4.2 s · Title screen. Low-poly sunset hills, a chrome bevelled "HIGH SCHOOL" with a lens flare sweeping across, and a blinking PRESS START. Scrolling on is pressing start.

Sound on the real site: CRT thunk, tray motor, disc click and whirr, the TV's high-pitched whine on power-up, a short memory-card blip, and a title sting. All original.
