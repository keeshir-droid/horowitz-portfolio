The system that holds it together

Every achievement is an “unlock”

The worlds change completely from chapter to chapter, but the way you earn credit never does. That consistency is what makes a patchwork of media styles feel like one story, and it's how a skimmer gets the facts.

Tier A · Headline (its own scene)The big ones: the $60 → $16K brand, the 21 projects, Adobe, the motor thesis, the visa moment, martial-arts gold. The scene dramatizes it and the unlock card lands with the number.
Tier B · Standard (a beat inside a scene)Honor societies, carbon-neutral project, the pitch. A short moment plus a compact unlock card.
Tier C · Collected (a quick “ping”)Subject awards, lesson plans, theater and dance. They pop in as a short row, and all of it goes into the trophy case.
PERSISTENT HUDChapter + age, an XP bar that fills as you unlock things, the unlock count, an always-visible “☰ Index” escape, and mute.
PROOF CARDEvery unlock has: short title, one number, one line, and a “view proof ↗” link (live demo, photo, certificate, article) when we have it.
TROPHY CASEEverything unlocked collects in a case you can open at any time. It links straight into Index mode, filtered. The story and the Index are one set of data, so they never disagree.

Chapter 3 · ~2:05 · High school

The widest chapter. The kid is alone at boarding school and gets to try everything, so each scene gets a different medium: letters, guild boards, a living diorama, an engineering diagram, a rhythm game, a comedy special, a terminal.

LOOKMixed on purpose: each scene is a different medium, but all share a cool “blue-hour” palette and a quiet, intimate camera, so it reads as one lonely-then-growing stretch of life.

Why it feels like one story

Three devices that tie it together

The bookend. We open by going into a Game Boy screen. We close by coming out of a laptop screen into the real city. The kid you started with becomes the person you are now.
The echoes. The Game Boy chime returns as a chord. The “gym badges” return as the 21-project Pokédex. The stage and the spotlight from Chapter 1 return at the pitch.
