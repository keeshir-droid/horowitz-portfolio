Part 1 · the idea

A cutaway of your school. The windows light up one by one.

The camera starts outside a dark school building at night, in cutaway like a dollhouse. One window is lit: your arrival. As you scroll, the camera glides in, and every room you reach is a different medium for a different part of your life. The next room's window is already glowing before you get there, and you hear a sound from it first.

Why this holds attention: you always know where you are (one building, not eight unrelated pictures), you always see what's coming (the next lit window), and every room ends on a sound cue that belongs to the next room: the hum of a motor, a keyboard clatter, a crowd, a paw scratching at a door.

Part 3 · new and rebuilt rooms

Key frames

5 · Game room. The bracket is the whole scene: lines draw round by round while a crowd murmur grows, ending on one final. It shows you ran the thing, rather than telling anyone. Game name and number of players are [NEEDED].

6 · Auditorium. Replaces the three text cards. Three spotlights switch on one at a time, and each lands a note. Together the three notes make one chord, which is the payoff you hear. Labels are my draft wording of your message.
