## Aesthetic review of Chapter 1 (first 10 seconds)

Strengths: a coherent, warm Game Boy palette; the street (brick, doors, lamps, kerb, road dashes) is tidy and the parallax gives depth; the props read instantly and are charming (the easel painting, the chess piece bobbing, the robot with its antenna); the title card is clean and the dusk/lamps ending is a nice touch; the tone is humble, as briefed.

Weaknesses a reviewer will notice:
1. **The kid is small and low-contrast.** At 1280x720 he is ~11x20 logical pixels with hair and jacket in the same darkest shade; the head merges with the horizontal wall base band, and "brown skin" cannot be read in a 4-green palette. Suggest: make the sprite ~1.5x larger (e.g. 16x28), outline him in shade 0 and use shade 2/3 for the tee so the silhouette separates from the wall, add a lighter hair highlight (shade 1) and a 2-px visible tee/jacket collar; consider an accent-coloured tee (the one spot of non-green is not possible, so use the lightest shade for the tee).
2. **Skin tone/identity.** Keep the green constraint but show brown through contrast: skin = shade 1 against a shade-3 sidewalk, hair shade 0, never the same shade as the wall behind him. Put him on a lane with a clear lighter band behind the head.
3. **Dusk is noisy.** The dithered dusk (p 0.9-0.97) turns the sky and brick into speckle; it looks dirty rather than evening. Use a clean two-step palette shift (every shade one step darker, no per-pixel Bayer on the whole frame) and keep dither only for the lamp glow.
4. **Phone composition.** The scene occupies only ~35% of a 390x844 screen; the rest is flat dark-green bezel (large, empty). Either scale up the logical scene to fill more height (crop sky) or place the title/hint text in the bezel so the empty space has a purpose.
5. **Trophy shelf** has a stray "Z"-like glyph and an unclear sleeping shape; make it clearly "a few small cups and a rosette" (and drop the cat, or draw it clearly). Placeholder polaroid: the visible text "PHOTO HERE / CHILD" is truncated and awkward; show "PHOTO" and "(coming)" or hide the polaroid until a real photo exists (a "labelled placeholder" is required, but it should not read as finished UI).
6. Building seams: narrow dotted vertical edges at building joins (dither strip) read as glitches at 1280 px; use solid pilasters.
7. The kid walks through props (overlap) when passing; draw props on a lane behind him with a small offset.
8. The pixelate transition uses navy/white blocks, off the green palette; use palette-matched blocks for this chapter.

No error handling around the lazy `StoryMode` import in `src/App.tsx`: if the chunk fails to load (stale deploy: after `dist` was rebuilt, a page loaded with the old index chunk went blank and the HUD never appeared; reproduced during this run) the user sees a blank screen with no way to the Index. Fix: error boundary around `<Suspense>` with a "See the Index" card, or reload on chunk-load error.
3. The kid's legibility (see the aesthetic review): sprite too small and low-contrast; "PHOTO HERE / CHILD" placeholder text is truncated. This is the first thing a reviewer sees; I flag it should-fix on quality grounds.
4. The unlock toast (6.5 s) covers the SKIP CHAPTER button and the right dock on desktop and phone. Fix: raise the dock above the toast zone on the right or move the toast above the dock (`bottom: 80px`).
