Chapter 2 · concept v2 · deck-of-cards style

Draw a card

I read "deck style" as a deck of trading cards: each of the four middle-school achievements is a card you draw, flip and read, with an art window where your real photo goes. Quick concept frames, not the build. The card art here is placeholder.

If you meant something different by "deck style" (for example a slide deck, or a different kind of card game), tell me and I'll redo it. Total length is still about 20 seconds, and it opens out of Chapter 1's black "CHAPTER 2" title card.

Beat 1 · the deck

Four cards, fanned out

The four cards fan out face-down on a dark felt table, with the karate card lifting first. Scrolling draws them one at a time.

Beat 2 · the karate card

Flip it, read it, stamp it

1 · The card flips face-up with a holo-foil shimmer. Your gi photo goes in the art window (placeholder here). Its "move" is the achievement.

2 · A GOLD stamp slams onto the card with a small impact and a sparkle. The result card appears.

Beat 3 · the other three cards

Same card, different art

Each card is drawn, flipped and read in about 3-4 seconds. Photos go in the art windows later; for now they show simple illustrations.
