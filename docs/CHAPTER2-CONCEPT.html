Chapter 2 · concept frames · for your approval

Choose Your Fighter

Quick concept frames, not the build. The look is a bold arcade fighting-game screen (Tekken-style HUD, speed lines, big type), a sharp change from Chapter 1's soft pixel town, so you feel the jump from childhood into the competitive years. The gi photo is a placeholder silhouette until you send one.

Total length about 20 seconds. The karate fight gets about 9 s; the other three fighters about 3-4 s each. The chapter opens straight out of Chapter 1's black "CHAPTER 2" title card.

Beat 1 · the select screen

Four fighters, one cursor

The cursor steps across the four portraits as you scroll. Each one plays its own short moment. Karate goes first and gets the full fight.

Beat 2 · the karate fight

Round 1 → K.O. → gold

1 · "ROUND 1 — FIGHT!" with health bars. Your side shows the gi photo (placeholder here); the opponent is a faceless silhouette labelled THE FIELD.

2 · A hit-stop flash and a slow-motion zoom into "K.O." with a speed-line burst.

3 · "WINNER" and a gold medal drops in with the result card: 4th International Karate Championship · 2018 · Seisenkei Shitoryu.

Beat 3 · the other three fighters

Quick cards, same arcade look

Each takes 3-4 seconds: a curtain opens on a crowd, a ballot box fills, a circuit board comes alive. One compact "unlock" per card, no fanfare.
